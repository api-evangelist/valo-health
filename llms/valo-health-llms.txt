## About

Valo Health organizes its work around two stated capabilities:

- **Human Causal Biology** — applies AI/ML and statistical genetics to large-scale human datasets, then validates findings in human tissue.
- **Closed-Loop Chemistry** — pairs integrated computational modeling with laboratory work to engineer novel small molecules.

The company was launched out of Flagship Pioneering and is historically associated with its Opal Computational Platform. It operates a shared risk-and-reward partnership model with pharmaceutical companies, advancing early-stage R&D while partners lead clinical development.
